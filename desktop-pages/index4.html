Use a public username or URL only. Do not enter passwords, private messages, verification codes, or other sensitive information.

How to confirm an account

  1. Open the organization's official website yourself and follow its social links.
  2. Check the platform's own verification indicator and what it means on that platform.
  3. Compare the exact username, spelling, website link, and contact details.
  4. Be cautious about urgent payment requests, login links, prizes, or requests for security codes.
  5. If uncertain, contact the organization using details from its official website.
